K&K bot privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

K&K bot ("K") is a private assistant used only by the owners of one rental building and their building manager. It is run by the owners for themselves.

What it handles

  • Messages and photos posted in the owners' LINE group (bills, payment slips, short notes) and in the owners' private chat with the bot.
  • Bank statements the owners send it, read on the owners' own server. Lines that do not concern the building are dropped after reading.

It keeps rooms, amounts and dates. It does not keep ID card numbers or phone numbers; a payer is stored as a bank name and the last 4 digits of the account.

Google user data

  • K asks for one Google permission, drive.file. With it, K can only see and change files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in the Drive.
  • K uses it to create one folder, to save the original bill photos there, and to save a daily backup of its own data there.
  • K writes the confirmed figures to the owners' Google Sheet through a separate service account that the owners share the Sheet with.
  • Google user data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. Its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Services that process data for K

  • LINE (delivers the messages).
  • OpenAI's API (reads the numbers on photos of bills and slips; bank statements are never sent to it).
  • Google (the owners' Sheet and Drive, and the server K runs on).

Keeping and deleting

Records are kept for as long as the owners need them for the building's accounts. The owners can delete K's folder in their Drive at any time, and can remove K's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
