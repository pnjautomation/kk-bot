K&K bot

K&K bot (shown in LINE as "K") is a private assistant for the owners of one small rental building in Thailand and their building manager. It is not a public service: nobody else can use it, and it never talks to tenants.

What it does:

  • Reads the bills, payment slips and notes the building manager posts in the owners' LINE group, and asks her to confirm each reading.
  • Keeps the confirmed figures in the owners' own Google Sheet.
  • Saves the original photos and a daily backup in a folder it creates in the owners' Google Drive. It can only see files it created itself (Google's drive.file permission).
